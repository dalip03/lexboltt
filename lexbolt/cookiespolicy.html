This Cookie Policy explains how LEXBOLT AI LTD, (“we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookie and similar technologies on our website, www.lexbolt.ai (the “Website”). It also explains your choices regarding these technologies. Our registered address is 450 Bath Road, West Drayton, England, UB7 0EB.

What are cookie?

cookie are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or other electronic device) when you visit our Website. cookie help the Website recognize your device and store information about your preferences or past actions.

Types of cookie We Use

Strictly Necessary cookie

These cookie are essential for you to browse our Website and use its features, such as accessing secure areas. Without these cookie, services you have asked for (like logging into your account) cannot be provided.

Analytics/Performance cookie

We use an analytics tool to collect information about how visitors use our Website. These cookie collect information in an anonymous form, including the number of visitors to the Website, where visitors have come from, and the pages they visited. The information gathered helps us understand how visitors interact with our Website and improve its functionality and user experience.

Functionality cookie

These cookie allow the Website to remember choices you make (such as your username, region, or preferred language) and provide enhanced, more personal features. They may also be used to provide services you have requested, such as watching a video or commenting on a blog post.

Targeting/Advertising cookie

In some cases, we may use cookie to deliver content or advertisements relevant to your interests on our Website or on third-party websites. These cookie remember that you have visited our Website, track your browsing habits, and may share this information with other organizations or advertisers. (If you do not use certain categories of cookie—like targeting/advertising—feel free to remove those sections.)

How We Use cookie

  • Remember your preferences and settings;
  • Understand and analyze how our Website is being used;
  • Improve our Website’s performance and user experience;
  • Deliver relevant content and advertising (if applicable).

Third-Party cookie

Some cookie on our Website may be placed by third parties when you visit. These third parties may be suppliers who partner with us to deliver our services, enable features or functionality, or help us analyze how our Website is being used. These third parties include but are not limited to providers of external services like web traffic analysis services and advertising networks. Third-party cookie are governed by the respective third-party’s privacy or cookie policy.

Cookie Banner and Consent

When you visit our Website for the first time, you will see a cookie banner that offers the option to continue browsing or manage your cookie preferences. By continuing to use the Website, you agree to the use of cookie, except where prohibited by law. You can also opt out at any time as described below.

Managing cookie

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookie. Most web browsers automatically accept cookie, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline cookie if you prefer. You can also delete cookie that have already been placed on your device.

  • Browser Settings: Consult your browser’s “Help” or “Settings” menu for instructions on how to block or delete cookie.
  • Opt-Out: You may also opt out of certain analytics and advertising cookie. To opt out of our analytics tool or other cookie, please send an email request to hello@lexbolt.ai with your preference.

Please note that if you choose to reject or remove cookie, this may affect certain features or services of our Website.
